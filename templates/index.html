How A Project Runs with Us

From your initial brief to on-site handover, every stage is managed seamlessly in-house to deliver stress-free exhibition execution.

01

Brief & Strategy

We analyze your booth dimensions, hall layout, sightlines, target audience, and product display requirements to establish clear objectives.

02

3D Architectural Design

Our spatial designers build photorealistic 3D renders with lighting, graphics, and ergonomics, iterating until you give 100% approval.

03

Workshop Fabrication

In-house carpentry, metal framing, fabric lightbox printing, and pre-assembly electrical checks are completed prior to venue dispatch.

04

Supervised On-Site Handover

Our on-site project managers handle venue entry formalities, build-up, touch-ups, and supervised dismantling after show close.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our exhibition stall design, fabrication, and event management process.

What services does Exhibition Guru provide?
Exhibition Guru offers end-to-end corporate event and exhibition solutions: custom 3D exhibition booth design, workshop fabrication, double-decker mezzanine stalls, luxury pavilions, high-resolution printing, audio-visual stage setups, and on-site event management across India.
Which cities and trade centers in India do you cover?
We execute projects PAN India! Our primary exhibition hubs include Delhi NCR (Pragati Maidan & India Expo Mart), Mumbai (NESCO & Jio World), Bengaluru (BIEC), Hyderabad (HITEX), Pune, Ahmedabad, Chennai, and Kolkata.
How long before an exhibition should we book our stall design?
We recommend starting 3 to 5 weeks prior to the show dates to allow sufficient time for 3D iterations, organizer approvals, workshop fabrication, and pre-assembly quality checks. However, express execution is also available for urgent timelines.
How do I request a custom 3D design and quotation?
Simply fill in the inquiry form below, call our direct line, or message us on WhatsApp with your stall dimensions, venue name, and event dates. Our design team will get back to you within 2 hours with a costed proposal.
